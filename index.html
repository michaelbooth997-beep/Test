Meet Your Surgeon

Michael Booth, M.D.

Dr. Michael Booth is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Orthopaedics at The Ohio State University, where he specializes in adult reconstruction surgery — including total hip and total knee replacement.

A graduate of SUNY Upstate Medical University, Dr. Booth completed his orthopaedic surgery residency at West Virginia University and went on to train at one of the nation's premier institutions: Massachusetts General Hospital (MGH) in Boston, where he completed a dedicated Adult Reconstruction fellowship focused on hip and knee arthroplasty.

Dr. Booth is an active researcher with over 20 peer-reviewed publications in leading orthopaedic journals. His work focuses on improving value in joint replacement, optimizing surgical technique, and enhancing patient outcomes — research that directly informs how he cares for every patient.

Services & Procedures

Hip & Knee Replacement Procedures

Dr. Booth specializes in the full spectrum of hip and knee arthroplasty, from minimally invasive primary replacement to complex revision surgery.

Total Hip Replacement

Restoring mobility and eliminating hip pain

Conditions Treated

  • Hip osteoarthritis
  • Avascular necrosis (AVN)
  • Inflammatory arthritis (rheumatoid)
  • Post-traumatic arthritis
  • Hip dysplasia

Approaches & Techniques

  • Anterior, posterior, and lateral approaches
  • Cemented and cementless fixation
  • Minimally invasive techniques
  • Revision & complex reconstruction

Total Knee Replacement

Returning you to an active, pain-free life

Conditions Treated

  • Knee osteoarthritis
  • Inflammatory arthritis
  • Post-traumatic arthritis
  • Severe knee deformity
  • Failed prior knee surgery

Approaches & Techniques

  • Cemented and cementless TKA
  • Unicompartmental (partial) knee replacement
  • Medial UKA for isolated medial disease
  • Revision total knee arthroplasty

Partial Knee Replacement

Targeted relief with bone-preserving surgery

Who Is a Candidate?

  • Arthritis confined to one compartment of the knee
  • Intact ligaments and healthy cartilage elsewhere
  • Patients seeking faster recovery
  • Younger, active patients with focal disease

Benefits

  • Smaller incision, faster recovery
  • More natural knee feel and motion
  • Bone and tissue preservation
  • Option for future total knee if needed

Revision Joint Replacement

Expertise when your first replacement needs attention

Common Indications

  • Implant loosening or wear
  • Periprosthetic joint infection (PJI)
  • Instability or dislocation
  • Periprosthetic fracture
  • Component failure or malposition

Dr. Booth's Approach

  • Thorough pre-operative workup and planning
  • Access to full range of revision implants
  • Multidisciplinary infection management team
  • Complex bone reconstruction expertise
